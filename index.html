Guild Flip Kit: buy cheap green gear, sell it at its real price

Green gear with a random suffix ("of the Tiger", "of Shadow Wrath") is often listed at the price of the plain item, because the game fills in the cheapest price of any suffix. Some suffixes are worth far more. This kit tells TradeSkillMaster (TSM) which exact pieces to look for, what to pay at most and what to sell for. Built 2026-11-18 21:25 from 162 hourly snapshots of the US PvP Horde auction house.

1. Import it into TSM (once)

  1. Copy one import below (button). Start with tier 1, add tier 2 when you want more. Tier 3 is large and optional.
  2. In game: type /tsm, open Groups, click the import icon, paste with Ctrl+V (a big paste takes a few seconds).
  3. Tick include operations, then Import. You get a group Guild Flips with the tier inside, split by value. Import each tier the same way: they go into the same Guild Flips group.

Or download them as text files: tier 1, tier 2, tier 3.

2. Buy

  • Sniper (TSM tab at the auction house, Sniper): it watches the newest listings and shows any piece of the kit listed at or under its buy price. Click to buy. Good deals vanish fast: most are bought by someone within the hour.
  • Shopping: select Guild Flips > 1 Top 10 percent in the group list and search. It shows only listings at or under the buy price. Search the top tier, not the whole group: the whole group is tens of thousands of pieces and takes very long.
  • With 5 to 20 gold: buy pieces of 1g or less, many different ones rather than several of the same, and keep about 2g for listing fees.

3. Sell

  • TSM Auctioning, Post scan with the Guild Flips groups selected. It lists each piece for 24 hours at about 85% of its value, or matches a cheaper copy down to a floor.
  • Not sold after a day: post again. After two failed listings, lower the price by hand or sell it to a vendor. A failed 24 hour listing costs a deposit (about 60% of the vendor price). Do not use TSM's NumExpires for an automatic markdown: it counts the failed listings of every suffix of the item together and can count one failed listing several times.

The tiers

How the prices work. TSM prices every suffix version at the price of the plain item: its dbmarket, the "TSM price" below. The pieces in this kit are the suffixes worth well more than that. Each group holds pieces worth about the same multiple of the TSM price, and its operations are written as that multiple: for example "buy at 1.3x or less" and "sell at 2.4x". A piece is posted first at about 85% of its value, and TSM matches a cheaper copy of the same piece down to about 65%. So when TSM's desktop app updates TSM's prices, the kit's buy and sell prices move with them. Keep the TSM desktop app running.

"Promise" = how often a piece was listed cheap enough to buy (at or under half its value and at most 3g, last 3 days) times the chance it sells within a day at 80% of its value, for pieces that leave at least 20s profit.

What to expect

A reality check from one player who has run this for a week (pieces that cost 1g or less, two days or more after buying): 832 pieces, 94% sold, typical price paid 25s, cash back so far 49s a piece after the auction cut and the price paid. Half of the sold ones sold within 4 hours. With 10g that is roughly 20 to 30 pieces at a time, so a few gold of profit a day if you catch deals regularly.

Limits

  • We all buy from the same auction house. Every guildmate using this competes with the others for the same cheap listings.
  • Prices follow TSM's data, the groups do not. Which multiple a piece is worth was measured on 2026-11-18 21:25. That changes slowly. Ask for a new import every few weeks and import it with "move already grouped items" and "replace existing operations" ticked.
  • The TSM price is for the plain item. If many valuable copies sell, the plain price rises and the kit's prices with it, which is right. A sudden odd TSM price (a few strange sales) moves the kit's prices too.
  • Values are estimates. About 30% off is typical for a single piece. The 2x rule leaves room for that.
  • Bands, not exact prices. Every piece in a group shares one multiple. A piece at the top of its group sells a little cheap, one at the bottom a little dear.
  • No markdown on relists. TSM does not know how often your own piece failed, so lower it by hand or vendor it.
  • One click, one purchase. Nothing here buys by itself. Sniper only shows the deals.
